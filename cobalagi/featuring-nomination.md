# Coba Lagi: App Store featuring nomination

Drafted 2026-10-30, for 2.9.0. The user submits it in App Store Connect → Apps → Coba Lagi → Featuring → Nominations. The form is in English; check each field's limit there before pasting.

Every claim is from the released App Store listing (the app repo's `store/app_store/en-US/description.txt`). The regional languages of Indonesia are **not** in 2.9.0 (decided 2026-10-24, texts and voices still need a native speaker's review), so this nomination doesn't mention them. They get their own nomination below once that version is ready.

## Nomination 1: now (the released app)

- **Type:** App Enhancements (the closest fit for an app that is already live; there is no new version to launch).
- **Name:** Coba Lagi: coding before reading, in Indonesian and English
- **Countries or regions:** Indonesia, then all others where it is sold.
- **Description:**

> Coba Lagi ("try again") is a coding game for children aged 6–8. Kids put picture blocks in order to guide Koba the robot across nine planets, one coding idea per planet: directions, sequencing, loops, building their own block, checking ahead, variables, debugging, repeat-until and if/otherwise. Every instruction is spoken aloud in Bahasa Indonesia and English, so children who can't read yet can play; readers can switch to typing the same program as code.
>
> A short warm-up game finds each child's starting point, and after every puzzle the app picks what comes next: the next planet, a generated practice puzzle (always checked to be solvable) or a review of an earlier skill. Mistakes are met with encouragement, never "wrong", and the block that caused a bump is marked.
>
> It is free for one player, with no ads, no tracking and no accounts, and it never connects to the internet. Parents get a gated parent area with break reminders and progress reports. It runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac.
>
> There are few coding apps for young children in Bahasa Indonesia. Coba Lagi is made in Indonesia for Indonesian families first, with English side by side.

- **Supporting link:** https://cobalagi.ardeman.com
