# Coba Lagi and Hour of Code

Checked 2026-10-30.

## Where it stands

- Code.org renamed the event **Hour of AI** in 2025. Classic Hour of Code activities stay listed in their own catalog. CS Education Week is in December. Code.org hasn't published the 2026 dates yet; school calendars give Dec 7–11 or 7–13.
- **The activity submission window is closed** (hourofai.org/faq). The 2025 cycle closed on Oct 1, 2025, and the FAQ doesn't give a date for the next window. Questions go to hourofai@code.org.
- So the plan's "submit before Dec 7" can't happen this year. Instead, email to ask (1) whether a late or rolling listing in the Hour of Code catalog is possible and (2) when the 2027 window opens.

## How Coba Lagi fits (from the older Hour of Code guidelines)

Good fit: beginner programming, no sign-up or payment, a non-English language, directed puzzles rather than an open-ended challenge, not gender-specific, and ages 6–8 (K–2).

Ranks lower: activities that need an install. Coba Lagi has no web version, and web or smartphone-friendly activities are preferred.

Asked for: an activity link that leads to `code.org/api/hour/finish` when a learner finishes, or an "I'm finished with my Hour of Code" button. Coba Lagi has neither, since the app has no internet permission. Ask whether a store-link listing can skip this; don't change the app first.
